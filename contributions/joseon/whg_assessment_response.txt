Thank you very much for sharing the sample datasets, and for the clear
introduction to Team Yeokji (역지사지) and the two databases. I have now done a
full technical inspection of the three shapefiles, building on Palak's initial

===================================================================
1. WHAT WE RECEIVED
===================================================================
A single ZIP (whg_sampledata_20260716) containing three ESRI shapefiles, each
complete with .shp/.dbf/.prj/.shx/.cpg and ArcGIS .shp.xml metadata:

  (a) kr_admin_1864_lv2_pg  - 335 POLYGON features: level-2 administrative
      districts (군현 / counties) of Joseon.
  (b) kr_admin_1864_lv2_pt  - 344 POINT (PointZ) features: 335 county seats
      (읍치, eupchi) + 9 provincial governor's offices (감영, level-1).
  (c) kr_placename_ddy_all_pt - 989 POINT features: place names and map symbols
      transcribed from the Daedongyeojido (大東輿地圖). We understand this is a
      sample from the full 15,529-point database.

===================================================================
2. WHG'S INDEPENDENT TECHNICAL ASSESSMENT
===================================================================

PROJECTION (important - please confirm)
  All three layers are in EPSG:5179 (Korea 2000 / Unified Coordinate System,
  Transverse Mercator, central meridian 127.5, GRS80), NOT geographic WGS84.
  We verified this reprojects correctly: coordinates fall across the whole
  Korean peninsula (approx. 124.5-132.3 E, 33.1-42.9 N). WHG stores geometry
  as WGS84 lon/lat (RFC 7946), so we will reproject 5179 -> 4326 on ingest and
  round to 6 decimal places. No action needed from you, but please confirm
  5179 is indeed the intended CRS (the .prj is unambiguous, so we are
  confident, but it is worth a one-line confirmation).

ENCODING (good news)
  The .cpg files declare UTF-8 and all Hangul and Hanja decode cleanly - e.g.
  안동 / andong, 五峰山 / 문학산. This is better than the usual CP949 situation
  with Korean shapefiles and removes a whole class of risk.

ATTRIBUTE SCHEMA (as found)
  Admin layers (pg and pt share a schema; pg additionally has area_m2):
    OBJECTID, hisgeo_id, feature_cd, admid_grp, admid_gis, adm_level,
    base_year, time_begin, time_end, [area_m2], comment, src_gis, check_db,
    nm_kor, nm_eng, nm_engmr
  Placename layer:
    OBJECTID, hisgeo_id, feature_cd, gis_symbol, nm_kor, nm_chn, nm_eng,
    nm_engmr, loc_rank, loc_memo, loc_addr, src, check_db

  Names / scripts:
    - nm_kor  = Hangul (primary title)
    - nm_chn  = Hanja / Chinese characters (placename layer only, 988/989
                populated; the admin layers have NO Hanja field)
    - nm_eng  = romanization (Revised Romanization style, e.g. "cheongdo")
    - nm_engmr = McCune-Reischauer romanization (e.g. "ch'ongdo")
    Two romanization schemes plus Hangul (and Hanja for placenames) is exactly
    the multi-script name variation WHG is built to track.

  Feature types:
    - Admin polygons: feature_cd is uniformly 행정구역 (administrative district),
      adm_level "lv2".
    - Admin points: 읍치 (county seat, 335) and 감영 (provincial office, 9).
    - Placenames: a rich 25-category vocabulary - 산 mountain (253), 고개 pass,
      도서 island, 하천 river, 촌락 village, 고산성 old fortress, 역참 post
      station, 봉수 beacon-fire, 읍치 seat, 나루 ferry, 능침 royal tomb, 창고
      granary, 사찰 temple, etc. This maps well to WHG's type system (we would
      build a Korean-feature -> Getty AAT crosswalk, as we did for CHGIS).

  Identifiers:
    - hisgeo_id is POPULATED and unique in the placename layer (989/989 distinct,
      e.g. "경기_광주_오봉산(조선)" = province_county_name(dynasty)), but is
      "<reserved>" (i.e. NOT yet assigned) in BOTH admin layers. This is the one
      real gap for the admin data - see the questions below.
    - admid_grp is populated and unique in both admin layers (335 / 344 distinct)
      and is the reliable join key between polygons and points (see below).

TEMPORAL
  - Admin layers: genuine per-feature temporal fields. time_begin ranges
    1392-1823 and time_end 1859-1914 (distinct end years include 1866, 1895,
    1896, 1906, 1914 - clearly reflecting the 1895 reforms and 1914 colonial
    redistricting). There is also a base_year field listing multiple snapshot
    years as text, e.g. "s1432/s1530/s1765/s1789/s1864/s1895/s1910/s1912"
    (15 distinct patterns). These map naturally onto WHG timespans; the
    multi-snapshot base_year needs a modelling discussion (below).
  - Placename layer: NO per-feature dates. Temporality is only the dynasty
    marker "(조선)" embedded in hisgeo_id, and the source is dated by the map
    (Daedongyeojido, 1861 woodblock / late 19thC). We would apply a
    dataset-level Joseon timespan unless you have finer dating.
  - The ArcGIS .shp.xml holds only processing lineage (ArcGIS 10.8, edits
    2024-2026) - no title/abstract/rights/date metadata. The lineage does
    reveal the sample was spatially filtered to the Han River + East Sea
    watersheds ("한강수계인접/동해수계인접"), which explains why the 989
    placenames cluster in Gyeonggi and the east (approx. 126.0-127.8 E,
    36.9-38.4 N) rather than covering the whole peninsula.

GEOMETRY SANITY
  - All 335 polygons are geometrically valid (shapely). 133 of them are
    MultiPolygons - consistent with Joseon exclaves (월경지) and island
    territories; RFC 7946 MultiPolygon handles these cleanly.
  - The admin points are stored as PointZ, but Z is uniformly 0.0 (a container
    artefact, not elevation).
  - We confirmed all 335 county-seat points fall INSIDE their matching district
    polygon. So the point layer is not a mere label/centroid - it is the actual
    administrative seat (eupchi) within the district. Both representations are
    worth keeping.

RELATIONSHIP BETWEEN LAYERS
  - Polygons <-> points: a clean 1:1 join on admid_grp. All 335 lv2 seat points
    match a polygon; the 9 extra points are lv1 감영 (provincial offices) with
    no polygon. NOTE: do NOT join on admid_gis - it carries a founding-year
    suffix that differs between the two layers (polygon "경상_안동(1392)" may
    pair with a differently-suffixed point). The year-stripped key / admid_grp
    is the stable link.
  - Placenames <-> admin: the placename hisgeo_id encodes province_county
    (e.g. "경기_광주_..."), so a textual/spatial link to the admin districts is
    feasible, but there is no shared numeric key. We would derive the linkage
    at ingest.

===================================================================
3. WHERE WE AGREE / DIFFER WITH PALAK'S FIRST READ
===================================================================
Agreements (confirmed against the files):
  - Both datasets are well-structured and align closely with WHG. Confirmed.
  - The placename data carries Korean, Chinese and English forms. Confirmed
    (Hangul + Hanja + in fact TWO romanizations).
  - Feature-type classifications are detailed. Confirmed (25 categories).
  - 132 placename records are marked "impossible to locate" (불가). Confirmed
    exactly - loc_rank breaks down as 확실/certain 527, 유력/probable 240,
    불가/impossible 132, 추정/estimated 90.
  - Admin time_begin/time_end map onto WHG timespans, and base_year (multiple
    snapshots) needs a modelling discussion. Confirmed.
  - Shapefile is a format we can convert. Confirmed.

Points to add / refine beyond the first read:
  - PROJECTION: the data is NOT already WGS84 - it is EPSG:5179 and must be
    reprojected. This is the single most important ingest step and was worth
    flagging explicitly.
  - STABLE IDs: hisgeo_id is unpopulated ("<reserved>") in BOTH admin layers.
    The placename layer has stable IDs; the admin layers do not yet. WHG needs a
    persistent per-feature ID, so this is our main request for the admin data.
  - The 132 "불가" points still carry coordinates in the file - so they are
    plotted somewhere. We need to know whether those coordinates are best
    guesses/placeholders or should be treated as truly unlocated.
  - The admin layers have no Hanja (nm_chn); only placenames do. Adding Hanja to
    the district/seat names would make the WHG name-variant coverage consistent.
  - Coverage includes present-day North Korea (함경/황해/평안 provinces, up to
    ~42.9 N). WHG will assign ccodes KR and KP accordingly - please confirm you
    want the northern features included.
  - The loc_rank certainty grades and loc_addr (modern location) are valuable
    provenance we would preserve as record-level metadata.

Net verdict: SUITABLE for WHG ingestion as a new authority (working namespace
"joseon", modelled on our existing CHGIS integration, which your database design
already resembles). No blocking technical problems; the open items are
clarifications and a couple of small data additions, not rework.

===================================================================
4. QUESTIONS WE NEED ANSWERED BEFORE A FIRST INGESTION
===================================================================
  1. Stable IDs: can you populate hisgeo_id (or provide another persistent key)
     for the two admin layers? admid_grp is unique in the sample and could serve
     as an interim key - is it stable across your year-by-year database, or does
     it change when a district is created/renamed/abolished?
  2. The 132 "불가" placenames: include as unlocated records, or exclude from the
     first submission? And are the coordinates on those 132 placeholders or
     genuine best-estimates?
  3. base_year: the multi-snapshot list (e.g. s1432/.../s1912) - does each token
     mean "this district's boundary is attested/unchanged at that snapshot
     year", or does it enumerate distinct boundary versions? This determines
     whether we emit one timespan or several per district.
  4. Boundary vintage: the filename says 1864, but the polygons carry
     time_begin/time_end spanning 1392-1914. Does a single polygon represent the
     1864 snapshot specifically, or the district's whole life with 1864 as one
     reference year? (This affects how we label the geometry's validity.)
  5. Projection confirmation: EPSG:5179 (Korea 2000 Unified) - correct?
  6. The 9 lv1 감영 points have blank names (nm_kor = "-"). Can you supply names,
     or should we label them from admid_grp (province name + "감영")?
  7. Source / provenance: admin src_gis cites "국편 역사지리정보 DB 2024" (the
     National Institute of Korean History historical-geography DB) plus Team
     Yeokji boundary restoration; placename src cites 대동여지도 and related
     works. Should WHG attribute to Team Yeokji, to the underlying sources, or
     both, and is there a citation string you would like used?
  8. Licensing: WHG does not gate ingestion on licence terms, but for
     downstream attribution we should record how you would like this data
     licensed / credited. What terms apply?
  9. Full dataset preferred, and please do NOT pre-process on our account.
     Rather than iterating on this watershed-filtered sample (989 of ~15,529
     placenames), our strong preference is to ingest directly from the FULL
     databases - the complete placename set and the complete national admin set -
     in their native form. There is no need for you to reproject, convert the
     format, clip the extent, or otherwise prepare the data for us: WHG does all
     of that as a matter of routine (reprojection EPSG:5179 -> WGS84, shapefile
     conversion, script handling, etc.). The rawer and more complete, the better.
     The ONLY pre-processing we would ask you to consider is removing any facets
     you regard as sensitive or not yet suitable for open publication - beyond
     that, please send the data exactly as it sits in your system.

===================================================================
5. LIKELY INGESTION PATH IF WE PROCEED
===================================================================
Assuming the above is resolved, the route into WHG is straightforward and
mirrors our CHGIS pipeline:

  1. Reproject all geometry EPSG:5179 -> WGS84 (EPSG:4326), round to 6 dp
     (RFC 7946). Polygons and PointZ -> 2D.
  2. Namespaced place_id per feature (e.g. joseon:<hisgeo_id>). Admin districts
     keyed on the ID you confirm in Q1.
  3. Titles and toponyms[]: title = nm_kor; name variants =
     nm_kor (ko), nm_chn (Hani/zh-Hant, placenames), nm_eng and nm_engmr
     (both ko-Latn). These feed WHG's cross-script name matching.
  4. types[]: map feature_cd / gis_symbol via a Korean-feature -> Getty AAT
     crosswalk (we would draft it and share for your review).
  5. geometries[]: district polygon and its county-seat point both retained (the
     seat can be carried as a second geometry or as a related "seat of" record).
  6. timespans: from time_begin/time_end for admin (pending the base_year
     decision in Q3); dynasty-level Joseon span for placenames unless finer
     dating is available.
  7. ccodes: KR (+ KP for northern features, pending Q on coverage).
  8. Provenance: preserve src/src_gis, loc_rank, loc_addr, check_db as
     record-level metadata for attribution and confidence display.

We can validate the field mapping quickly on our side, then share the resulting
WHG records back to you for review. Our preference (see Q9) is to run this against
the FULL databases rather than the sample, in their native EPSG:5179 shapefiles -
there is no need to prepare a reduced, reprojected, or reformatted extract for us.

We are very happy with how clean and well-documented this material is, and we
look forward to discussing the open questions on the Zoom call.
